# TrackFlow public services

## About TrackFlow

TrackFlow is a third-party logistics provider founded in 2009. It provides warehouse management, last-mile delivery and reverse logistics for e-commerce brands. Its warehouse operations are staffed by over 130 logistics professionals.

## E-commerce specializations

TrackFlow specializes in fashion, electronics and cosmetics. It supports secure packaging, climate control and accurate inventory tracking.

## Shipping volume and enquiries

TrackFlow's full-service logistics offering is designed for businesses shipping at least 100 orders per month. For lower volumes, the team assesses suitability during the initial consultation. The commercial team responds to information requests within 24–48 business hours and arranges a discovery call. Requesting information does not establish a service agreement.

## United States public coverage

TrackFlow serves the United States from its Los Angeles warehouse with national coverage through UPS, FedEx and DHL.

## Spain public coverage

TrackFlow serves Spain from Zaragoza, including peninsular and island coverage through MRW, SEUR and DHL.

# Public delivery guidance

## United States domestic delivery

Within the United States, standard shipping is 3–5 business days. Express shipping is 24–48 hours and is available only in the Los Angeles metropolitan area. Actual service depends on the service contracted by the customer brand.

## Spain domestic delivery

Within Spain, standard shipping is 3–5 business days. Express shipping is 24–48 hours and is available only in the Zaragoza–Madrid corridor. Actual service depends on the service contracted by the customer brand.

## International delivery

International shipping between TrackFlow's operating countries is 7–12 business days, subject to customs. TrackFlow does not control customs hold times. This is general guidance, not a live shipment status or a guaranteed arrival date.

## Peak-date caveat

TrackFlow does not guarantee a delivery SLA on declared high-demand dates, including Black Friday, Christmas and January Sales. Delivery times may extend and should be communicated in advance. Contact TrackFlow for applicable service terms.

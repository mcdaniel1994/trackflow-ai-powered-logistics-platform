# TrackFlow Descript Recording Checklist

## Visual style system

- **Lower thirds:** Name and role only, with a thin deep-green underline. Show for 3–4 seconds on the first camera appearance; do not repeat on every cut.
- **Section titles:** Clean, left-aligned sans-serif with a deep-green accent line. Overlay them for 1.5–2 seconds so they do not add separate runtime.
- **Captions:** Keep high-contrast captions on throughout the video for accessibility.
- **Transitions:** Use straight cuts for most edits. Reserve 0.4–0.6 second crossfades for major section boundaries.
- **Camera PiP:** Bottom-right, approximately 22–28% of the frame, with rounded corners, a thin border, and a subtle shadow. Never cover the primary UI action.
- **Music:** Keep any ambient corporate bed very low under narration and near-silent during the CX and RFP details.
- **Pacing:** Hold camera shots for 8–15 seconds. During demos, change crops or visual focus every 3–6 seconds to match narration beats. Keep zooms slow and restrained.
- **Footage rule:** Generated or stock-style B-roll belongs only in the opening problem framing. From the public website onward, use real TrackFlow footage.

## Record as separate scenes

1. Warehouse B-roll: cold-open hook.
2. Camera: personal introduction and lower third.
3. Operational B-roll plus camera pivot: problem and opportunity.
4. Browser: public website hero, services, and coverage.
5. Browser: Login → Admin Demo → Backoffice Overview.
6. Browser: saved CX agent conversation.
7. Browser: completed Northstar RFP.
8. Camera: engineering philosophy and technical choices.
9. Browser: successful and rejected Agent OS runs.
10. Camera: one clip for each required 4Geeks answer.

Separate recordings make it easier to crop, zoom, remove loading time, and adjust pacing inside Descript.

## Before recording

- Rehearse aloud twice with a timer; target 4:50–5:05.
- Start the public-site recording at the top of the homepage.
- Use a signed-out or private browser window so the public-site Login click naturally reaches the sign-in page.
- Use the **Admin Demo** autofill control instead of typing a password on camera.
- Pre-open the completed Northstar RFP and useful Agent OS runs in separate tabs as backups.
- Use the existing CX conversation and completed RFP; do not depend on fresh AI output during the recording.
- Close notifications, unrelated tabs, password managers, bookmarks, and personal apps.
- Record at 1080p or higher and set browser zoom so text remains readable after Descript cropping.

## Descript layout guidance

- Use full-screen browser footage for the public website and primary product interactions.
- Use a small face-camera overlay only when it adds personality without covering important UI.
- Cut or speed up scrolling; keep the website overview to 20–30 seconds.
- Zoom into the exact CX answer, RFP approval state, or agent trace being discussed.
- Synchronize CX labels with the demonstrated behavior: **Policy Answer**, **Live Tool Routing**, and **Safe Refusal**.
- Synchronize RFP step labels with narration: **Upload**, **Route**, **Draft**, **Evaluate**, **Approve**, and **Assemble**.
- Remove login loading time, but keep the Login click and authenticated transition visible.
- Keep transitions simple; the product should remain the visual focus.
- Record several seconds of clean room tone for audio edits.

## Camera and audio

- Record horizontally.
- Face a window or lamp; avoid bright backlighting.
- Use a headset or earbud microphone in a quiet room.
- Look into the lens for the opening and all three closing answers.
- Pause for two full seconds before and after each Q&A response.

## Final review

- Runtime is approximately five minutes.
- The public website receives 20–30 seconds before the Backoffice login.
- App text is readable at normal playback size.
- No real passwords, tokens, private tabs, or notifications appear.
- The opening leads with the result before your name.
- The story moves clearly from public business promise to internal operational execution.
- The demo explains business value before technical details.
- The engineering section names real trade-offs and explains why you chose them.
- All three 4Geeks questions are answered in complete sentences.

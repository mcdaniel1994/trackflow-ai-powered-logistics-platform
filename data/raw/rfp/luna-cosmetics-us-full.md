# Request for Proposal — Third-Party Logistics

**From:** Luna Cosmetics, Inc. — Los Angeles, California, United States
**To:** TrackFlow — Commercial Team
**Prepared:** 2026-08-01

## About us

Luna Cosmetics is a direct-to-consumer clean-beauty brand shipping skincare and color cosmetics to
customers across the United States. We are outgrowing our current in-house fulfilment and are seeking
a logistics partner.

## What we are requesting

We would like a proposal covering:

- **Warehousing / storage** for our SKU catalogue, including inbound receiving and pick-and-pack.
- **Last-mile delivery** across the continental United States.

We do **not** need returns management at this time; we handle exchanges directly.

## Volume and expectations

- Estimated **~5,000 orders per month**, growing.
- Average 2–3 items per order.
- We expect a stated on-time delivery SLA and a volume-based pricing structure.

## Timeline

We are evaluating partners now and need a proposal within **20 days**. Pricing should be quoted in
**US dollars**.

## Reference budget

We are targeting a blended fulfilment cost in the low single-digit dollars per order but are open to a
structure that scales with volume.
